Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our collections, enquiries, and ordering process.

You can explore our latest collections directly on this website under the Collections and New Arrivals sections, or visit our official Instagram profile @vernica_fashions for daily styling updates.
Click the "Enquire on WhatsApp" button on any product card or use our WhatsApp buttons across the site. This will automatically open a pre-filled chat with our team where you can ask for exact pricing and details.
Size and color availability can vary by collection item. Simply drop us a quick message on WhatsApp mentioning the specific outfit you love, and our boutique staff will share all available variants.
Orders are coordinated personally through WhatsApp. Once you confirm your preferred product, size, and delivery address with our team, we will guide you through the final confirmation and payment arrangements.
You can contact us via our WhatsApp number, email, or through our Instagram profile @vernica_fashions. You can also use the contact form at the bottom of this page.
Delivery times and exchange guidelines are discussed directly during your WhatsApp order confirmation. Please feel free to ask our team about specific shipping arrangements for your location when placing an enquiry.
